# STAT 517: Statistical Inference

## Course Overview

- **Term:** Summer 2026
- **Instructor:** Dr. Jin Hyung Lee ([lee6024@purdue.edu](mailto:lee6024@purdue.edu))

## Course Description

This course covers mathematical statistical inference and its applications. Topics include probability review, confidence intervals, hypothesis testing, properties of estimators, maximum likelihood inference, sufficiency, loss and risk functions, minimum variance unbiased estimation, and Bayesian inference.

The course is fully online over 8 weeks, with weekly lecture modules, homework assignments, online discussions, a midterm exam, and a final exam.

## Topics Covered

- Review of probability, random variables, transformations, and expectations
- Multivariate random variables, conditional distributions, dependence, and correlation
- Sampling distributions, t and F distributions, and confidence intervals
- Hypothesis testing procedures and p-values
- Order statistics and chi-square tests
- Monte Carlo methods
- Convergence in probability and distribution
- Consistency, Delta method, moment-generating-function techniques, and the Central Limit Theorem
- Maximum likelihood estimation and properties of MLEs
- Fisher information and Cramér-Rao lower bound
- Likelihood ratio tests and multiparameter inference
- Decision theory, sufficient statistics, factorization theorem, completeness, and MVUE
- Bayesian inference, prior/posterior reasoning, credible intervals, and Bayesian hypothesis testing
